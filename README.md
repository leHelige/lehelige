### `01 / ABOUT`

I work at the intersection of **security investigations, detection engineering, and defensive infrastructure**. My focus is hands-on **Digital Forensics & Incident Response**: examining evidence, reconstructing adversary activity, scoping incidents, and translating findings into actionable response.

Building and operating security telemetry pipelines gives me a practical view of the full investigative lifecycle — from **what gets collected** to **what the evidence can actually prove**.

> **My approach:** Evidence first. Clear hypotheses. Reproducible findings. No conclusions beyond the data.
